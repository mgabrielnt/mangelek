# Pricing — monochrome revision

## Scope

Navigation, one primary pricing section, FAQ, compact footer.
The primary section contains a short heading and one split package card.
Six feature labels replace grouped descriptions and repeated inclusions.
Domain costs and planned add-ons are explained in the four native FAQ items.
A dedicated navigation replaces the Home header on this route.
White canvas, charcoal purchase panel, neutral typography, thin borders.
Responsive stacking at 760 px; no fixed content heights.

## Commercial configuration

Approved billing totals belong in src/data/pricing/plan.ts.
Empty options show a pending price and a link to explore themes.
One option displays its price; multiple options expose native billing radios.
Annual amounts are the full annual bill, with the billing interval visible.
No prices, discounts, trials, or usage quotas are invented.
Approved limits render only when configured in src/data/pricing/features.ts.
FAQ explains that pricing, quotas, and terms precede payment availability.
Before launch, configure prices, entitlements, taxes, and subscription terms.
The current CTA navigates to the Home theme gallery; there is no checkout.
