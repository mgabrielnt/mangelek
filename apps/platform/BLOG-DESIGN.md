# Blogs surface

Mode: Read. Scope: /blogs and /blogs/[slug]. Continue the user's established code-led workflow and Mangelek identity.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A practical library for business owners preparing their website, with complete reading paths rather than decorative article cards.
OWN-WORLD: Local Manrope, white, ink #20242a, muted #606872, fine borders #dce1e6, restrained olive category text. Existing editorial product illustrations anchor the subject.
STORY: Find a relevant guide, read clear sections, follow the next step to explore themes or related guides.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Quiet site navigation, large left-aligned title and a featured article pairing generous image and copy. Below: query field, category controls and three remaining guides. Filters replace the featured arrangement with matching results.
FORM: Existing visual language extended to a reading surface. Mobile stacks featured image and text, wraps categories and keeps reading widths fluid. Article pages have a linked contents list, prose at about 68ch and related guides.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Content and interaction
Four original starter guides, no invented authors, publication dates, metrics or product capabilities. Content lives in typed local data; this is not a CMS integration. Estimated reading duration derives from text length. Query/category state lives in the URL. Visible focus, semantic links and headings, polite result announcements, useful empty state, and reduced-motion support.

## Asset provenance
Reused repository illustrations in public/images/marketing/features: website-editor.webp, cms-dashboard.webp, domain-management.webp, business-dashboard.webp. No image modification or external raster sourcing.
