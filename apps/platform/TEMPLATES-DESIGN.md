# Templates

Extension of the existing Mangelek visual system. Code-led Experience/Operate surface at /templates and /templates/[slug]. Visitors compare six existing concept artworks, filter by business category, and discuss a chosen concept through the existing contact flow. No claim that a concept is a working installed theme.

First viewport: large two-line Manrope heading, quiet supporting text, full-width category controls and two substantial visual previews. White canvas, ink typography, olive selection feedback. Signature interaction: restrained arrow translation on pointer hover; immediate filtering preserves focus and stores query/category in URL. Reduced motion removes transitions.

Content: existing data/templates.ts and original public/images/marketing/categories artwork. Full concept image on dedicated detail routes; native links preserve navigation and focus expectations. Mobile uses one column with 44px controls, 16px search, and wrapping category buttons. No fabricated popularity, authors, or usage metrics.

Quality bar: previews lead; controls remain clear; no horizontal overflow at 320px; meaningful empty state and reset. Use keyboard-operable native controls and distinct focus outlines. Finish with desktop/mobile inspection and independent review.
